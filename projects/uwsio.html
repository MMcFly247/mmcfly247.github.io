UW Silica Integrated Optics

Photonics Arduino Laser Systems Research Leadership

Overview

Co-founded an official University design team specifically aimed at Nanotechnology Engineering students. The team focuses on integrated photonics research and development, providing hands-on experience that bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical applications.

Leadership Role

As co-founder, I established the team structure, recruited members, and defined research objectives. Led the initiative to gain official university recognition and access to laboratory facilities at the University of Waterloo.

Technical Projects

  • Led research and fabrication of a high-speed programmable laser shutter system using Arduino and custom hardware components
  • Actively working in the optics lab creating DOEs (Design of Experiments) to test for optimal laser parameters for desired voxel creation
  • Developing techniques for silica-based integrated optical circuits
  • Establishing laboratory protocols and safety procedures for team operations

Impact

The team provides invaluable hands-on experience for nanotechnology engineering students, fostering innovation and practical skills development in the field of integrated photonics.
