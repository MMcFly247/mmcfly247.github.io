Transverse Flux Motor Development

Electric Motors Manufacturing Team Collaboration Hyperloop

Overview

Collaborated with Waterloop's propulsion team to design and manufacture Transverse Flux Motors (TFMs) for use in hyperloop technology displays. This project involved working with a multidisciplinary team to bring cutting-edge motor technology to life.

Technical Contribution

Developed a precision copper coil winding system to build electric motors, significantly enhancing manufacturing speed and accuracy. The system allowed for consistent, repeatable coil production critical for motor performance in hyperloop applications.

Team Collaboration

  • Worked alongside mechanical, electrical, and software engineers
  • Participated in design reviews and iterative prototyping sessions
  • Contributed to manufacturing process optimization
  • Helped establish quality control procedures for motor production

Technologies Used

CAD design, precision manufacturing, electric motor theory, collaborative engineering tools

September 2023 - January 2024
